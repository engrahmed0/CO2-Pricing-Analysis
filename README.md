# Impact of CO₂ Pricing on Residential Energy Systems

## 1) Project Overview
This project investigates the impact of different CO₂ pricing scenarios on residential energy systems. The study evaluates how carbon pricing influences technology selection, energy costs, emissions, and the economic feasibility of various heating and electricity supply concepts.
The project was completed as part of my Master's studies in Mechanical Engineering (Specialization: Sustainable Energy Systems) at Ruhr University Bochum.

## 2) Objectives
The project aims to:
- Evaluate the influence of CO₂ pricing on residential energy systems.
- Compare different heating and electricity generation technologies.
- Analyse technical, environmental, and economic performance.
- Identify cost-effective and sustainable energy supply concepts.
- Support engineering decision-making using quantitative analysis.
- 
## 3) Scope of the Study
The analysis includes:
- Residential building energy demand
- Heating technologies
- Electricity supply concepts
- Renewable energy integration
- Carbon emissions
- Economic evaluation
- Sensitivity analysis

## 4) Methodology
The project follows these steps:
1. Definition of residential energy demand.
2. Collection of technical and economic input data.
3. Development of different energy supply scenarios.
4. Evaluation under various CO₂ pricing levels.
5. Comparison of costs, emissions, and system performance.
6. Technical interpretation of results.
7. Recommendations for sustainable energy planning.
